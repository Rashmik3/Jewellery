About Sparkle

Passion after work – jewellery made at home

Hello, I'm the maker behind Sparkle

Sparkle started as a quiet evening hobby. After a full day of work I sit at my small home table and create jewellery – earrings, necklaces, bracelets and rings – one piece at a time.

Every item is handmade with care. I use quality materials, simple tools and a lot of love. No big factory, no mass production – just unique pieces that carry a little bit of sparkle into everyday life.

Whether you choose an existing design or request something custom, I am happy to create something special for you.
